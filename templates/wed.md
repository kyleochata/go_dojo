# Wednesday — Foundations Unit (30 min)

Label: LEARN. Drop day from week 6 onward (plan rule 11); in weeks
3–5 the drop day is Tuesday and Wednesday must run — it carries the
concurrency on-ramp that Thursday's hunts depend on.

Content by week: weeks 1–2 Big-O micro-unit; weeks 3–5 concurrency
on-ramp, failure-modes-first; week 6+ internals (stack/heap, value
semantics, escape analysis, slices, maps) alternating with
hypothesis-driven debugging.

## Part 3 — Post-test (5 min)

Re-run the same pre-test drill. Pass → unit closed; log it. Fail →
the unit is mis-scoped or unfinished: split it, log the split, queue
the remainder. A LEARN unit may burn one overtime token to close;
never a second in the same week (plan rule 2).

---

## 10-minute floor

Pre-test only. Attempt the drill cold, log pass (unit cut — a full
result, not a partial one) or fail (entry point recorded for the
next full Wednesday). No teaching on a floor day.
